THE CORE PRINCIPLES

The author's notes, verbatim. Every chapter after this is built on them.

"Breath — going to body"

Exhale as you drop level and commit to the body shots. Breathing powers the entry. The breath is always the first domino.

"Vision"

Eyes stay open and calm. See the punch coming; never turn away from the fight.

"Timing — back foot"

Feel the weight settle on the back foot. That loaded back foot IS your counter-timing.

"Make the other person miss while you get into a better spot to score points."

THE FIX — SAME ORDER

Long exhale. Small steps. Rhythm comes back. Soft eyes — see the man, not just the gloves. Between rounds: in for four, out for six or eight.

ONE JOB. NOT FIVE. ONE.

PRACTICE PROTOCOL

  1. SHADOWBOX THE SIX: run the moves in order — jab-and-angle, inches, slip-inside to body, weave, block/roll, body-head x3 — three rounds, small steps only.
  2. BREATH DISCIPLINE: between every round, in for four, out for six or eight. Never spend the minute on panic.
  3. ONE JOB: each round, pick ONE thing — breath, or steps, or rhythm, or soft eyes. Not five. One.
  4. BODY-HEAD REFLEX: after every slip or weave in practice, the three-punch combination comes out automatically.
  5. CHECK THE LIGHTS: when something feels off in sparring, check in order — breath, legs, timing, vision — and fix the FIRST dark light, not the symptom.
  6. RUN IT: in sparring, pick one opponent system per round to switch off — his breath, his legs, his timing, or his vision.
